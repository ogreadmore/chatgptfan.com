Microsoft says its services have recovered after an outage lasting more than a day disrupted Outlook, Teams and other products. The disruption also reached AI assistants: Reuters reports that OpenAI and Anthropic experienced Microsoft connector problems that were subsequently resolved. This was a failure of services those assistants depend on, not evidence that AI caused the outage. Microsoft said it had found no indication of malicious activity. [Reuters reporting](https://www.chinadailyhk.com/hk/article/642694)

OpenAI’s own incident record describes broken new Microsoft connections and tools that were not working as expected. It marked the Microsoft 365 connector incident resolved on November 13. The affected component was ChatGPT’s connectors and apps; the notice does not describe a shutdown of every ChatGPT conversation. Service-status figures are aggregate, so a resolved incident still leaves you needing to check your own task. [OpenAI incident record](https://status.openai.com/incidents/01M78GAT3AK9P9W8Z230KHFVE8)

If a request still fails, first try the information in its original service. Can you open the email or document there? Then check the account connected to ChatGPT and read the specific error. OpenAI’s troubleshooting guide separates availability, authorization, missing information and unsupported actions. It advises checking service status rather than repeatedly reconnecting an app that remains unusable. [Troubleshooting guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001497-troubleshooting-plugins-and-apps-in-chatgpt)

Account choice matters, especially if you use both personal and work email. A connection to your personal account cannot supply files that only your work account can access. Adding a Microsoft account also does not turn your personal ChatGPT workspace into a managed business workspace. If reconnection is actually needed, review the account and requested access; reconnecting one account does not update all the others. [Account guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001494-connecting-and-managing-app-accounts-in-chatgpt)

For a task that sends a message or changes a file, check the destination before retrying. An unclear result can mean some work already completed. Inspect sent mail or the affected record, then repeat only the work you can confirm is missing. If you cannot establish what happened, stop and take the observed error and timing to support. [Recovery guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001497-troubleshooting-plugins-and-apps-in-chatgpt)

Our takeaway for anyone building a recurring AI workflow: keep a way to inspect the underlying service, make incomplete results visible, and decide who handles an interruption. A fluent answer is a poor substitute for confirmation that the intended email was sent or the right file changed. The useful test of automation includes how it recovers when one of its dependencies stops working.
