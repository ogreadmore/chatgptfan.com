OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on November 13, giving its personal agents, called dots, a way to pay for a purchase after the user approves it. The initial rollout is limited to adults on ChatGPT Pro in the United States with eligible US-issued cards. American Express, Mastercard and Visa are the payment-network partners. [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/openai-launches-chatgpt-wallet-letting-ai-agents-pay-for-purchases-5026114)

Imagine asking a dot to find a sweater in your size and within your budget. It can compare options and prepare the purchase. You then review the seller, item, total, selected card and any shipping or recurring-payment terms. Approval requires a biometric check on a supported device. The help guide places this rollout in conversations with a dot and says ordinary Chat, Work and standalone Codex threads are outside its current availability. [Purchase guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001654-making-purchases-with-chatgpt-wallet)

After approval, the payment network supplies a substitute payment credential. OpenAI says the agent and merchant receive that credential while the actual card details stay hidden. The selected card is still charged, and its existing rewards and protections for eligible disputes continue to apply. [How Wallet works](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/)

Pay attention to what happens next. An approval screen means you have authorized the attempt. The merchant still decides whether to accept and fulfill the order, and the issuer decides whether to authorize the transaction. If the outcome is unclear, look for a merchant confirmation before trying again. That check can prevent an uncertain checkout turning into two orders. [Wallet terms](https://openai.com/policies/chatgpt-wallet-terms/) · [Checkout guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001654-making-purchases-with-chatgpt-wallet)

There is also a substantial data trail. OpenAI’s Wallet privacy notice describes collecting purchase details, contact and delivery information, approval records, and security signals. Removing a card can leave records retained for pending transactions, fraud prevention or legal obligations. Consider which purchases you are comfortable connecting to your AI account before enabling the feature. [Wallet privacy notice](https://openai.com/policies/chatgpt-wallet-privacy-notice/)

For an accepted order, the merchant handles returns, cancellations and refunds. The card issuer handles charge disputes. OpenAI Support is the route for a Wallet error or a purchase that differs from what you approved. Keep the merchant receipt and the approval record so you can explain the problem. [Support guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001654-making-purchases-with-chatgpt-wallet) · [Refund and dispute terms](https://openai.com/policies/chatgpt-wallet-terms/)

OpenAI also describes a future where users preapprove shopping within spending limits, categories or time windows. Those are plans. Today’s launch asks for approval purchase by purchase, making the details on that screen the part of the shopping task that still needs your attention. [Launch announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/)
