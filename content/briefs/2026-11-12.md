Ask ChatGPT to help choose between a cheap flight with a tight connection and a later nonstop, and you still hold the decision. Ask a personal AI assistant to sign in, spend your miles and issue a United ticket, and someone else gets a say: the airline. On November 11, United said it was restricting unauthorized third-party assistants on its own website and app and through travel agencies and booking platforms. [United’s announcement](https://united.mediaroom.com/news-releases?item=125496) · [Reuters reporting](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/united-airlines-moves-to-block-ai-agents-from-its-platform-over-security-concerns-5019752)

One example explains why the distinction matters. At check-in, a traveler has to acknowledge what is in their bags. An assistant might know the flight number, seat assignment and frequent-flyer balance. It wasn’t there when you packed. United also points to purchases, changes to reservations and redemptions of miles that a passenger didn’t intend. These are risks the airline gives for its decision, not a tally of incidents.

## Whose assistant is it?

Singapore Airlines offers a useful contrast. Its own Flight Recommender takes a request in ordinary language and looks up fares and availability. Its service chatbot, Kris, can answer questions about disruptions and hand a conversation to a representative. OpenAI describes both in a customer case study. The airline chose the tools, connected them to its systems and decided what they could do. That’s different from handing an outside assistant your airline password. [How the Singapore Airlines tools work](https://openai.com/index/singapore-airlines-travel-work/)

For a United trip, a useful handoff is a shortlist: flight numbers, connection times and the trade-offs you care about. Open the airline’s app or an authorized booking service, confirm the live fare and baggage terms, and make the purchase there. If an assistant already booked a ticket before the change, United told *PhocusWire* it would not cancel those bookings on that basis. Check the reservation against what you meant to buy.
