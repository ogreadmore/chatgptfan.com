Two changes to ChatGPT’s usage allowance are arriving close together. One corrects a launch mistake. The other changes how ChatGPT and Codex share a budget. For anyone planning a long task, the distinction matters more than another model announcement.

## A correction that makes the meter move faster

On November 26, OpenAI’s Tibo Sottiaux said GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol had been consuming less allowance than intended because of an accounting error. After the correction, the same work uses allowance 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol, according to the announcement reproduced by [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-warns-gpt-62-astra-will-hit-usage-limits-40-percent-faster-because-it-fixed-a-bug/) and [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/chatgpt/weve-fixed-this-now-so-the-same-work-will-use-more-of-your-plans-usage-allowance-openai-admits-it-made-a-mistake-when-launching-gpt-6-2-and-now-paying-chatgpt-users-could-hit-their-usage-limits-up-to-40-percent-faster).

The company said it would not charge past usage retroactively and offered one banked reset to Go, Plus, Pro and Business users. These are OpenAI’s stated accounting changes, not measurements of our own workloads. A faster rate of consumption also does not translate directly into the same percentage reduction in the number of tasks someone can finish.

## A shared allowance starts in December

Separately, [OpenAI’s usage guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001625-understanding-chatgpt-and-codex-usage-limits) says Plus, Pro and Business will share an allowance across ChatGPT and Codex starting in December, with a small early group before then. Consumption depends on tokens, model and task; Instant chat is excluded. Settings → Usage will show the combined balance and reset times.

OpenAI expects most users will not face more frequent waits. That is a company expectation, not a guarantee for a particular workload. The practical implication is that substantial coding work and other eligible ChatGPT work can draw on the same reserve.

## Before starting a large job

Check the allowance and reset times shown on your own account. Do not assume last week’s experience predicts how far the current balance will stretch.

If a banked reset is available, read its scope and expiration before spending it. OpenAI’s [reset documentation](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001516-managing-usage-with-gpt-6-astra-in-work-and-codex) describes a one-time refresh of covered limits, not a permanent increase in capacity. An unfinished task still needs its result checked after work resumes.

**Our view:** predictable limits are part of a usable product. People should be able to tell what changed, what work remains possible and when access returns without piecing together posts and support pages. The useful test of an AI subscription is completed, checked work within a budget—not a model name or a temporary reset.
