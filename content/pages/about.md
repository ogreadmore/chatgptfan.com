# About

ChatGPT Fan is an independent publication and resource library for people interested in ChatGPT and the wider world of AI.

We publish a daily brief, link to original reporting, and collect useful learning materials, projects, and safety resources. Enthusiasm for a tool should never make its maker immune to criticism.

## Independence

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI. ChatGPT and OpenAI are trademarks of their respective owner.

There are currently no paid placements or affiliate links on this site. If that changes, placements will be labeled.

## How we publish

News feeds link to publishers’ original articles. Feed headlines are attributed to their source; a company announcement, independent report, and community discussion are different kinds of evidence.

Briefs and resource descriptions may be researched and drafted with AI assistance. Sources accompany substantive claims. We distinguish reporting, opinion, and advocacy, and publish corrections in the affected article. Automated preparation is not a guarantee of accuracy: the underlying sources remain available for you to check.

## Contact & privacy

For corrections, send the article URL, the statement at issue, and a supporting source through Contact. Substantive corrections are noted on the affected article; minor spelling and formatting fixes may be made without a separate note. For copyright or other rights concerns, identify the material and your relationship to it so we can investigate.

Questions, corrections, and resource suggestions are welcome through [Contact](../contact/). See our [privacy page](../privacy/) for contact processing, optional analytics, and the preferences stored on your device.

Our [Terms of Use](../terms/) explain content reuse, external links, and the limits of the information we publish.
